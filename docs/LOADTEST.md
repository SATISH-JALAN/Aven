- **Run:** 2,318 swaps over 250 blocks, up to 16 per block. Both directions, a quarter of them exact-out. The oracle moves ±4% and the mark ±0.8% every block, and ~15% of blocks get a broken read (revert, gas burn, empty or >uint64 return).

  - **0 reverts** (I1);
  - **0 fee violations** (I2: in [min, max] or exactly fallback);
  - **0 zero-fee swaps** (I3);
  - **0 top-of-block violations** (one top price per block);
  - **0 anchor violations** (the anchor moves at most once per timestamp);
  - 387 fallback-fee swaps, all 387 of them under broken reads.
- **Gas per swap** (full swap through the test router: router + PoolManager + hook): p50 **174,880**, p95 **187,767**, max 214,024. The hook's own share is ~47k (PROOFS.md).
- **Gas per block** of those swaps: p95 2,780,006, max 3,024,869, against the 3,000,000 HyperEVM small block.
- **Finding: capacity, not a bug.** One small block fits **~16 Aven swaps** (vs ~24 plain swaps at ~123k). Extra swaps land in the next block 1 s later; fees stay correct because top-of-block is per block.

## Findings and fixes

1. **Fixed: stale block number (found by 7.1(b) and 7.2).** viem caches `getBlockNumber()` for ~4 s by default.
   - **Impact:**
     - (a) `verifyAll` / `pnpm verify --all` run right after a burst computed its scan end from a stale head and found **0 of 280** events;
     - (b) the Arena poller (every 500 ms) saw only ~1 block in 4: **10 bot iterations over 74 blocks**.
   - **Fix:** `cacheTime: 0` in `verifyAll`, the verify CLI client, the Arena client and the local-chain client.
   - **Regression tests:**
     - `verifyAll asks for the live head (cacheTime 0)` (`backend/core/test/chain.test.ts`);
     - `public client never caches the block number` (`backend/arena/test/chain.test.ts`).
     - Both fail when the fix is removed (mutation-checked).
   - Every number above is from the final run, after all fixes.
2. **Fixed: slow receipt polling (found by 7.2 after fix 1).** `waitForTransactionReceipt` polls at the client's `pollingInterval`, which viem defaults to 4 s.
   - **Impact:** with 1 s blocks every bot trade waited up to 4 s for its receipt, so a two-pool iteration took 8 s+. Measured: **11 iterations over 75 blocks** under API load.
   - **Fix:** `pollingInterval: 250` on the Arena clients.
   - **Regression:** `backend/arena/test/chain.test.ts` asserts the 250 ms interval.
3. **Fixed: /state thundering herd (found by 7.2).** The per-block cache was filled only after the computation finished, so on every new block all concurrent callers recomputed /state (~10 RPC reads each). Measured: **p99 1,188 ms**.
   - **Fix:** one in-flight computation per block, shared by all callers; failures aren't cached.
   - **Regression:** `backend/arena/test/state.test.ts` (50 concurrent calls → 1 computation; mutation-checked: without the fix it's 50).
4. **Accepted: block capacity.** ~16 Aven swaps fit one 3M-gas small block (full swap ~175–188k gas, the hook ~47k of it). Extra swaps spill into the next block; correctness is unaffected.
5. **Accepted: shocks fail while the RPC is down.** The Arena reports the error to the admin and doesn't retry a shock forever. The bot loop itself retries with backoff and resumes when the RPC returns.
6. **Accepted: a simulated swap can revert when the price moves between planning and simulation** (seen as `swap reverted` in the error sample during the shocks). The iteration records it and the next block re-plans; no transaction is sent for a failed simulation.
